This site sets no cookies, shows no ads and has no accounts or forms.

## Visitor counts

I use [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/), an open-source analytics service, to see which pages people read. For each page view it records the page address, the referring site, the browser and operating system, the screen size and the country. It does not store IP addresses and does not track anyone across sites.

The site also sends a few anonymous events to the same counter: clicks on the resume, email and outside links, which parts of the campus get opened, how far people get in the guide and the games, the address of any page that was not found, and the file and line of any script error. The text of an error message is never sent.

## Stored in your browser

The campus remembers a few things in your browser's local storage so they survive a reload: your theme choice, whether you have seen the guide, game scores and difficulty, and achievements. If you allow your location for the sky, the sun position is worked out in your browser, a rounded copy of the location is kept there, and it is not sent anywhere. Clearing your browser's site data removes all of it.
