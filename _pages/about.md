Hi, I'm Paawan! I graduated in May 2025 with a Bachelor of Science in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the [University of Texas at Austin](https://utexas.edu), where I specialized in Software Engineering and Design and completed a minor in Entrepreneurship. I am also a proud recipient of the [Bostick Francis Scholarship for Electrical Engineers](https://www.statesman.com/obituaries/p0157680).

I'm currently in Cohort 7 of Gauntlet AI, where I've shipped fifteen production-style AI systems against real briefs, and I'm building [Company Agent Layer](/portfolio/company-agent-layer/), which warns the right teams, down to the field, before a pull request breaks a service that depends on them. Alongside that, I train and evaluate frontier AI models as a contract engineer for Handshake AI, Micro1 and Mercor, across electronic design automation, code generation and API behavior.

Before this, I was an AI & Data Analyst at SkyTerra Group, an AI, ML, and engineering-focused consulting group in the energy space. I worked across two areas there: data pipelines that carried readings from healthcare wearables to the cloud, and the field integration of off-grid hybrid power systems.

Across school and work, I’ve built projects in AI agents and their security, small-model fine-tuning, biometric signals, cryptographic hardware, geospatial analytics, financial modeling and energy systems. Along the way I’ve won hackathons, led my senior design team and worked directly with consulting clients.

Beyond engineering, I co-founded Fundare Finance, where I developed a SaaS dashboard for small-business revenue tracking, and I’ve contributed to education as a mathematics tutor and teacher. I also led and performed with a nationally competitive Bollywood fusion dance team.

Outside of agents and models, I spend my time on entrepreneurial projects and mentoring students. Feel free to explore my work and connect with me on [LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com/in/paawan-desai) or [GitHub](https://github.com/paawandesai).
